# Welcome to Corner 💜

Corner is your space for projects and to-dos: thinking webs, a Today page, a weekly review and more.

**It's private.** Everything is locked on your device with *your* passphrase before it's saved. The person who set this up can't read it, the database company can't read it, and no AI can read it.

---

## First time (about 3 minutes)

1. **Open the link** you were sent, for example `https://taylanewman.github.io/corner/`.
2. **If it asks for two codes** (*Project URL* and *key*): paste the ones you were sent. This is once per device.
3. **Create your account** with your email and a password. This just lets you into your space.
4. **Create your passphrase.** This is the real key to your data.
   - Use **4 or more random words**, like `violet teapot river moon`.
   - Write it down somewhere safe (a password manager is ideal).
   - ⚠️ **If you forget it, nobody can recover your data.** Not even the person who set this up. That's what makes it private.
5. You're in! Some sample tasks are there to show how things work. Delete them anytime.
6. Want your own name at the top? Open **Settings & backup** and change the title.

---

## Make it feel like an app

- **Mac (Safari):** with Corner open, go to **File → Add to Dock**. It gets its own icon and window.
- **iPhone:** in Safari, tap **Share** (□↑) → **Add to Home Screen**.
- **Android (Chrome):** tap **⋮** → **Add to Home screen**.

---

## Your other devices

Open the same link on your phone or another computer, **sign in** with the same email and password, and type your passphrase **once** on that device. That's it.

Changes show up on your other devices **live**, within a second or two, while they're open.

---

## The 🔒 button (bottom right)

| | |
|---|---|
| **Download backup** | Saves a copy of everything to a file on your device. Do this every couple of weeks: a pink dot reminds you. To restore one, open the backup file in a text editor, copy all of it, and paste it into **Settings & backup → Restore from a backup**. |
| **Lock this device** | Forgets the passphrase on this device. Use it on a shared or borrowed computer. |
| **Sign out** | Signs out on this device. Your data stays safe in your space. |

If the 🔒 has an **orange ring**, you're offline. Keep working: your changes sync when you're back online.

---

## Good to know

- **No AI is connected to Corner.** You add tasks yourself.
- **Updates arrive automatically.** Refresh the page, and your data is never touched by an update.
- **On iPhone, keep it on your Home Screen.** Safari can clear website data for pages you haven't opened in a few weeks. Your data is still safe in your space. You'd just sign in and type your passphrase again.
